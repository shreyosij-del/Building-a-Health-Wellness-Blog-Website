# Building-a-Health-Wellness-Blog-Website
Health is Wealth is a digital resource center dedicated to helping individuals turn daily habits into a healthy way of living. The platform provides practical, evidence-based tools to help users achieve a balanced lifestyle through consistency rather than drastic, unsustainable changes.



Key Website Features

Healthy Recipes: Curated meal ideas, including quick breakfasts, wholesome lunches, and specialized plans for vegan or gluten-free diets.




Fitness Routine Guides: Home-based, equipment-minimal workouts categorized for beginners to advanced users.




Habit Tracker Templates: Customizable daily, weekly, and monthly trackers to monitor progress in nutrition, productivity, and mindfulness.


Productivity & Detox: Strategies for digital detoxing and energy management to prevent burnout.


Target Audience (User Persona)
The project identifies Ishani Malhotra as a primary buyer persona:


Demographics: 28-year-old mid-level accountant living in Kolkata.


Lifestyle: Busy schedule, balancing work and personal commitments.


Goals: Building sustainable fitness habits and learning quick, healthy cooking methods.


Challenges: Time management, digital distractions, and confusion from conflicting health advice online.


Platform Philosophy
The project is built on four core pillars:


Balance Over Extremes: Finding flexible solutions that work for individual lives.


Mind-Body Connection: Integrating physical health with mental well-being.


Habit First: Prioritizing the building of routines as the driver for lasting results.


Community Support: Leveraging collective challenges and shared motivation.
